    Fit to the listener

    Same news. Different briefing.

    Each listener gets different stories, order, depth, length and language, chosen by cheap rules from their profile. Try your own with a wildcard listener.

    Making sense of the news

    From a pile of articles to ranked stories.

    Articles about the same event are grouped into one story. German and Dutch translations are folded into their English original, near-identical copies are merged, and stories are ranked by importance and coverage.

    Scalability and cost

    The large model only writes the script.

    Free signals decide which stories are worth a model call, so extraction stays the same size whether a day has a hundred articles or ten thousand. A small model extracts facts and checks claims; the large model only writes one script per listener.

    Path to product

    Built for a newsroom's morning.

    A publisher runs it once a day on its own feed. Editors review only what the verifier flags. Listeners always hear that the voice is synthetic.

    Who pays

    Publishers first

    A white-label daily briefing for publishers and brokers, priced per listener. At about $1 per listener per day, most of it voice, a subscription add-on or a sponsored slot covers it.

    • Publisher licence: per-listener audio for their readers
    • Listener: premium tier of an existing subscription
    • Advertiser: one disclosed sponsor slot per edition
    Newsroom workflow

    Runs before the editors wake up

    • 05:30: the overnight feed is grouped, extracted and ranked
    • 05:45: a script per listener is written and checked
    • Flagged segments are repaired automatically, or held for an editor
    • 06:00: audio and show notes with every source go out
    Disclosure

    Never pretends to be human

    • Every edition opens with a spoken AI-voice disclosure, in the listener's language
    • Show notes list every claim with a link to its article
    • No investment advice: blocked by rules and by the verifier
